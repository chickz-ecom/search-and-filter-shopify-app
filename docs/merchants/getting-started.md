# Getting started with Search & Filter

Written for: merchants installing the app.

## 1. Turn on the app embed

In the app, open **Home** and select **Turn on the app embed**. The theme editor opens on **App embeds**: switch on **Search & Filter**, then **Save**. Your search box now shows instant results as shoppers type.

Nothing changes on your storefront until you do this.

## 2. Add the blocks

- **Search page:** in the theme editor, open the **Search** template, select **Add block → Search results**, then **Save**.
- **Collection pages:** open a **Collection** template, select **Add block → Collection filters**, then **Save**.

The blocks keep your theme's own results underneath. If the app is ever unavailable, shoppers still see products.

Block settings: columns, products per page, filter position (left, top, drawer, or none if your theme provides its own) and whether to show the sort menu.

## 3. Tune search, filters and sorting

| Page | What you can do |
|---|---|
| **Search** | Choose which fields count most (title, SKU, tags, metafields…), how forgiving typos are, what happens to sold-out products, and whether to search collections, pages and articles. Try a search before saving. |
| **Sorting** | Turn sort options on or off, rename them, change the order and pick defaults, also per collection. |
| **Filters** | Add filters for price, availability, brand, type, options (size, color), tags and metafields. Choose how each one looks, and override them for specific collections. |
| **Merchandising** | Synonyms, redirects (for example "shipping" → your shipping page), words to ignore, and rules to pin, hide or boost products. |
| **Appearance** | Colors, spacing, texts in each language and, for advanced users, card templates, with a live preview. |

Changes reach your storefront within a minute.

## 4. Watch what shoppers search for

**Analytics** shows top searches, searches with no results and how shoppers use filters and sorting. For a search with no results, add a synonym or redirect in one click, or approve a suggested synonym.

## Prices and markets

Prices on search and collection pages come from Shopify for each shopper's market and currency, including fixed market prices. If a price can't be confirmed, the product shows "View price" rather than a wrong amount.
